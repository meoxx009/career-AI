# CareerAI — Product Requirements Document

Status: approved design brief for implementation, not a finished application. Owner: project author. Working product name: CareerAI (not trademark-checked).

## 1. Product promise

**From “What should I prepare?” to one clear, evidence-based next step.**

CareerAI helps Indian CS/IT students preparing for their first internship or software job understand role requirements, assess selected skills, choose a direction, and practise with a realistic plan. It is a preparation assistant, not a recruitment decision-maker, psychometric test, or placement guarantee.

### Inputs and decisions
- Confirmed visual direction: linen `#FAF3E1`, cotton `#F5E7C6`, tangerine `#FF6D1F`, charcoal `#222222`; tall display type; dark orange-glow background.
- Confirmed delivery: coded web app built with Antigravity and free tools; PRD, architecture, rules, design, tasks, memory and UI/UX rules files.
- Chosen starting scope: individual student, three entry-level roles, English UI with Hindi-capable fonts.
- Persona: Rahul, fictional 3rd-year CS student with a small Python project, limited SQL experience, and 6–8 available study hours per week.

## 2. P0 Requirements
- P01 Guest demo + account boundary
- P02 Account and privacy
- P03 Onboarding (01/03, one question per card)
- P04 Diagnostic (18 questions across six skills, save & resume, accessible untimed mode)
- P05 Role comparison (Backend, Frontend, Data Analyst, assessed alignment, evidence coverage, known gaps)
- P06 Roadmap (Prerequisite order, weekly hour budget, deliverables)
- P07 Progress persistence
- P08 Resume workspace (source facts, accept/edit/reject, unsupported claim warnings)
- P09 Interview practice (rubric checklist, AI-optional, text-first)
- P10 Failure handling (deterministic fallback mode, empty/loading/error states)
