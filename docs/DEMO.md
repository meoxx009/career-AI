# CareerAI — Hackathon Demo Script

Duration: 3 minutes. Demo data: synthetic Rahul. Say “fictional demo data” on screen and in narration.

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook

“Rahul has a degree, a Python project and some SQL knowledge. His problem is not a lack of tabs open; it is not knowing which next action is worth his limited time.”

Show landing. Say the product does not promise a job; it makes readiness and next steps visible.

## 0:20–0:45 — Quick profile

Enter third-year CS/IT, 6 study hours/week, interests in logical problem solving, location optional. Point out optional data and privacy copy.

## 0:45–1:10 — Diagnostic

Answer 3–4 representative questions. Say the full flow is 18 questions and save/resume exists. Show “diagnostic estimate” and not a certification.

## 1:10–1:35 — Explainable paths

Show Backend Developer, Frontend Developer and Data Analyst. Select Backend.

Say: “The score is not a placement probability. It is a versioned assessed alignment with coverage. The card shows what evidence was used and what remains unknown.”

Point to Python/logic evidence, API/testing gaps and source/version label.

## 1:35–2:00 — Roadmap

Show four weeks sized to six hours/week. Mark a REST API task done. Point out that progress is plan completion, not a claim of mastery.

## 2:00–2:30 — Truthful resume lab

Paste the synthetic resume and target description. Show matched terms and a missing term. Show an unsupported “1000 concurrent users” claim being flagged. Edit it to a fact-based sentence. Say: “Our AI rewrites evidence; it is not allowed to invent metrics.”

## 2:30–2:50 — Interview practice

Answer the technical question in text. Show fixed rubric feedback and one next action. Say text is a first-class mode; no camera or accent scoring in the MVP.

## 2:50–3:00 — Reliability close

Turn off/deny AI or simulate timeout. Show deterministic checklist mode and preserved draft. Close with:

“CareerAI does not sell certainty. It gives a student an explainable plan, a safe way to tell their story and one measurable next step.”

## Judge questions to prepare

- **Where is the AI?** Optional structured feedback adapter; deterministic scoring and safety checks remain in our code.
- **How do you prevent hallucination?** Source facts, fact IDs, confirmation gates, schema validation and a no-AI fallback.
- **How do you validate role requirements?** Versioned editor-curated seed data now; source-backed review workflow before public market claims.
- **Can it scale?** User-owned Postgres rows with RLS, small server-side functions, rate limits and provider abstraction. Scale claims are future work.
- **Why not a job portal?** The first bottleneck is clarity and preparation; opportunities come only after the readiness loop works.
